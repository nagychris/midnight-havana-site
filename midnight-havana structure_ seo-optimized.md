## **Meet Our Salsa Teachers**

Our teachers bring different backgrounds, styles and strengths to Midnight Havana, but share one idea: salsa is about more than memorising figures.

It is about understanding the music, moving naturally and actually dancing with the person in front of you.

### **Yago**

Yago combines salsa with a strong sense of music, movement and dynamics on the dance floor.

In 2025, he won the Son Cubano Competition at the Guaguancó Festival together with Helen. His dancing is musical, clear and strongly influenced by Cuban movement culture.

At Midnight Havana, Yago teaches Cuban Salsa and Rueda de Casino. His classes go beyond steps and combinations, focusing on rhythm, natural movement and dancing together.

**His motto: Understand the music. Feel the movement. Dance together.**

### **Helen**

For Helen, great salsa happens when musicality, connection and dancing together come naturally.

In 2025, she won the Son Cubano Competition at the Guaguancó Festival together with Yago. Her dancing is characterised by musicality, expression and a strong sense of connection between partners.

At Midnight Havana, Helen teaches Cuban Salsa and Rueda de Casino. Her classes are not just about learning figures, but about listening to the music, reacting to your partner and developing a more natural feeling for Cuban Salsa.

**Her motto: Hear the music. Feel the connection. Dance together.**

### **Sassan**

Sassan lives and breathes Rueda de Casino and has spent many years exploring how creative, playful and versatile Rueda can be.

As part of the SalsaNor Rueda Family, he regularly teaches at the international SalsaNor Rueda Congress, bringing his experience to dancers of different levels.

At Midnight Havana, Sassan combines solid Rueda technique with creative structures and the unique energy that appears when individual couples become one Rueda.

Expect to experiment, laugh and occasionally give your brain a workout.

**His motto: Discover, play and develop Rueda together.**
